# Historical Workbook Data Contract

This contract governs the fourteen supplied competition workbooks and every dataset generated by
`scripts/history_importer.py`. Its purpose is to make the historical matches useful for
training—especially the half-time/full-time (HT/FT) target—without leaking post-match or
unverified late-market information into pre-match features.

The importer accepts one workbook for each supported worksheet name:

| `日职` | `japan_j1` | `japan-j1` |
| `美职联` | `usa_mls` | `usa-mls` |
| `英超` | `england_premier_league` | `england-premier-league` |

The expanded bundle targets 2020–2026 histories for all fourteen competitions, including
Finland Veikkausliiga. Do not copy a row total from an older export into analysis or
documentation. The authoritative total and per-league/per-season counts are the values in
the current validated `manifest.json`; a partial 2026 count is expected to change as the
as-of date advances. Historical coverage makes competition-specific training possible; it
does not by itself prove live betting value or authorize a formal HT/FT or corner pick.

Each workbook must contain exactly one supported competition worksheet with at least one
finished match. Its first 87 columns and two header rows remain the strict football/HTFT
schema: the first row defines merged market groups and the second row defines individual
fields. A workbook may then append exactly the registered twelve corner-audit columns listed
below and may include the auxiliary read-only worksheets `角球盘口` and `数据质量`. Renaming,
reordering, inserting an unregistered column inside the first 87 columns, or adding an
unrecognized worksheet fails the import.

The twelve optional main-sheet corner-audit columns, in order, are:

```text
Titan比赛ID,全场角球主队,全场角球客队,全场角球总数,全场角球差,
半场角球主队,半场角球客队,半场角球总数,角球数据状态,角球结果来源,
角球采集时间,加时口径状态
```

Missing half-time corner counts stay blank; they must never be filled with zero. Full-time
corner counts mean verified regulation-time counts including stoppage time and excluding
extra time unless the source explicitly proves another settlement scope. The two auxiliary
worksheets are user-facing audit views only. `history_importer.py` reads the competition
worksheet's first 87 columns for football/HTFT outputs and validates the registered appended
header/sheet structure, but does not turn the twelve corner columns or auxiliary sheets into
HT/FT features. The appended columns use the merged top header `角球审计` followed by blank
top-header cells, matching the workbook schema.

The importer also fails closed on:

- any Excel formula, even when the cell contains a cached value;
- non-continuous source identifiers or a blank row inside the data region;
- a league value that differs from the worksheet name, or a status other than `完`;
- missing/equal team names, malformed or implausible scores, and total-goal conflicts;
- source `半全场` or `胜平负` labels that do not agree with the score;
- duplicate fixtures; and
- kickoff time moving backwards within a season, an invalid calendar rollover, or an
  unsupported/implicit timezone; and
- an unregistered appended column or worksheet.

Strings are trimmed and normalized to Unicode NFC. This does not silently merge aliases or
translated team names; a future alias table must be explicit, versioned, and tested.

## Kickoff and season rules

`比赛时间` stores only `MM-DD HH:MM`, while `年份` is the competition season rather than a
guaranteed calendar year. The operator must therefore supply the source timezone explicitly.
The default, `Asia/Shanghai`, matches the provenance assumption for the supplied export; do
not reuse it for another source without verification.

Within each season, the importer starts in the season year and applies an explicit
per-competition calendar policy. Calendar-year leagues never infer a rollover. European
autumn-to-spring competitions may cross once from October–December into January–March.
Brazil 2020 permits only its documented December-to-January delayed-season transition, and
AFC 2022 additionally permits its exceptional August-to-February transition. Every inferred
transition is written to `manifest.json`; an unapproved transition, more than one transition,
or any resulting backwards timestamp is rejected.

The normalized time fields mean:

- `source_kickoff`: timezone-aware local kickoff reconstructed from the workbook;
- `source_timezone`: the explicit operator-supplied IANA zone;
- `kickoff_utc`: the same instant in UTC; and
- `date`: the UTC calendar date used by the existing score-model interface.

`season` always preserves the source season label. Never reconstruct chronological order by
concatenating `season` and `比赛时间` outside this importer.

## Generated datasets

Run the importer into local ignored runtime storage rather than committing the supplied raw
workbooks or row-level exports:

```text
python scripts/history_importer.py SOURCE_OR_DIRECTORY \
  --output-dir .codex/soccer-predict/datasets \
  --source-timezone Asia/Shanghai \
  --as-of-date 2026-08-03
```

For each league it writes two CSV files and one bundle manifest.

### `*-scores.csv`

The score file contains:

```text
date,home_team,away_team,home_goals,away_goals,
half_home_goals,half_away_goals,half_result,full_result,htft_result,
league_key,league,season,competition_regime,format_version,phase_group,
season_status,round,source_row,
source_kickoff,source_timezone,kickoff_utc
```

Only `date`, `home_team`, and `away_team` are pre-match input fields in the minimum model
contract. Goals and result codes are supervised outcome labels. They must never be supplied
as features for the same fixture.

The normalized result codes are `H` (home win), `D` (draw), and `A` (away win):

- `half_result` is derived from the half-time score;
- `full_result` is derived from the full-time score; and
- `htft_result` concatenates them into one of the nine targets `HH`, `HD`, `HA`, `DH`,
  `DD`, `DA`, `AH`, `AD`, or `AA`.

For example, 0-0 at half-time and 2-1 at full-time becomes `half_result=D`,
`full_result=H`, and `htft_result=DH`. The source text label is used only as an integrity
cross-check; the normalized target is always re-derived from verified scores.

These labels make the data suitable for chronological HT/FT training and calibration. They
do not by themselves authorize an actionable HT/FT recommendation: promotion still requires
future clean live-forward validation, calibration across all nine classes, adequate coverage,
and pre-kickoff market evidence under the active policy.

`competition_regime` is a dataset-context boundary, not a team-strength feature. Regular
seasons use `regular`. Japan J1 fixtures inside the configured 2026 regional and placement
tournament window (through 6 June) use `2026_vision_regional`; the importer applies that label to both score and
opening-market rows. Read the exact included/excluded count from the current manifest and
registry rather than fixing it in documentation. The J.League's official special-season
overview describes the regional groups, while its regular schedule lists the later 2026/27
J1 season separately:
[`special format`](https://www.jleague.jp/special/2026specialseason/j1/),
[`regular schedule`](https://www.jleague.jp/en/j1/match/).

`format_version`, `phase_group`, and `season_status` are audit boundaries rather than
same-match features. The import keeps every collected Titan stage and its format/phase/status
labels visible, including Korean regular/championship/relegation splits, European/AFC
qualifying and main phases, format transitions, and playoffs. Retaining those rows does not
mean the current manager fits a separate production-strength model for every stage. A value
such as `partial_as_of_2026-08-03` marks a right-censored season snapshot; it may contribute
completed historical outcomes to a later-dated research fit, but it cannot be used as
model-promotion evidence.

### `*-opening-markets.csv`

The market file contains one row per match and bookmaker, limited to the source columns marked
as opening full-time 1X2, Asian handicap, and total-goals prices. Completeness flags are
included because missingness is systematic for some bookmakers and years. Do not mean-impute
those gaps.

The workbooks do not record the exact snapshot collection time. Consequently these prices are
`research_only_untimestamped_opening_snapshot`: they may serve as a benchmark or a separately
reported experiment, but they are not automatically joined into the production score model.
The files contain no first-half 1X2 odds and no nine-way HT/FT odds, so they cannot establish
HT/FT value or expected value on their own.

All closing 1X2, Asian, and total prices are excluded from every generated CSV. They are
validated only structurally and listed as quarantined in the manifest. Rankings, source
total/result labels, and other post-match-derived fields are likewise excluded as training
features.

## Independent corner-training source

The workbook corner columns and auxiliary sheets are convenient Excel audit/export views;
they are not the authoritative corner-model training input. Build league-scoped corner CSVs
from the collector's source-bound `corner_history.json` with:

```text
python scripts/corner_history_dataset_builder.py \
  --input .codex/soccer-predict/corner-history-expanded/corner_history.json \
  --output-dir .codex/soccer-predict/datasets/corner-history-expanded \
  --as-of-date 2026-08-03
```

The builder admits only complete, non-conflicting regulation-time corner results, preserves
missing/conflicting/extra-time-ambiguous counts in its own manifest, and hashes both source
evidence and generated CSVs. `corner_model_manager.py` then fits and backtests those exact
CSV artifacts. Never train the corner model by copying values back out of the presentation
workbook, and never merge corner result fields into football/HTFT same-match features.

The end-to-end order—from offline schedule normalization through resumable collection,
single-company research prices, sequential fourteen-league registry writes, final workbook
export, and HTFT re-import—is documented in
[expanded-history-runbook.md](expanded-history-runbook.md). Preserve every collector error
as missing evidence; no workbook or training transform may replace a missing corner count
with zero.

## Manifest and reproducibility

`manifest.json` records:

- importer and schema versions;
- source filename and SHA-256 hash;
- output filename, row count, and SHA-256 hash;
- league and season counts, format/phase/status cohorts, UTC date range, and every calendar rollover;
- per-bookmaker opening-market completeness;
- the feature whitelist, outcome-label fields, research-only fields, quarantined fields,
  caveats, and deterministic bundle hash.

Regenerate the bundle after any source change instead of editing a CSV by hand. A different
source hash, timezone, schema, or importer version is a different dataset lineage and must
produce new validation evidence.

## Training and evaluation boundary

Train separate league models; do not pool disconnected competitions as if team strength were
on one scale. The registered manager uses `regular-only-production-v1`: only
`competition_regime=regular` enters registered fitting or fixed-season scoring, while special
regimes retain only exclusion counts for drift auditing. Read `candidate`/`shadow` from the
current hash-bound registry instead of assigning statuses by league name. Registered
artifacts remain `formal_htft_eligible=false` until clean live-forward evidence exists. Use expanding-window or rolling
chronological evaluation, keep complete kickoff groups together, and reserve a genuinely
unseen final period for a one-time promotion decision. Newly promoted or unseen teams require
an explicit fallback and separate reporting; the manager fails closed by default.

For HT/FT, report at least nine-class log loss, multiclass Brier score, per-class support and
recall, calibration, and coverage/abstention. Because rare classes can make raw hit rate look
better or worse by chance, a higher historical win rate alone is not sufficient evidence.
Compare against simple league-frequency and independent-half baselines, then retain a change
only when it improves a genuinely unseen forward sample without a material calibration or
data-quality regression. The current historical cohorts remain component/development
evidence rather than an untouched end-to-end test of
`probability_top2_v3_post_selection`. Incomplete `partial_as_of_*` cohorts are always
research/shadow evidence and never promotion evidence.

All unfinished 2026 `partial_as_of_*` cohorts remain research/shadow, regardless of their
observed scores or apparent hit rate. They must never make a promotion gate pass.

The workbooks contain neither timestamped pre-kickoff nine-way HT/FT prices nor complete
live-forward betting records. Accordingly the evaluator can compare probability quality,
Top-1/Top-2 coverage, format cohorts, and phase cohorts, but cannot validate executable HT/FT
EV or ROI from these files.

The fixed-season evaluator binds each artifact to the importer manifest, score-file hashes,
opening-market hashes when enabled, and fixture identities. Non-promoted fit or bootstrap
parameters require `--experimental-override`; those runs receive configuration-experiment
split roles and cannot be presented as promotion evidence. When loading a saved artifact,
validate it against the exact dataset directory or manifest so source binding is checked, not
only the outer JSON hash.

Historical rows are valid training evidence, not strict out-of-sample betting records. Keep
the old prediction/review cohort for audit and learning, but never blend it with forward
predictions when claiming live win rate or ROI.
